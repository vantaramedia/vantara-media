Creator Rules & Partnership Guidelines

Welcome Creators

Vantara Media works with creators to build genuine brand partnerships. Please follow these guidelines to maintain quality and trust.

1. Content Guidelines

  • Create original and engaging content.
  • Content should follow platform community standards.
  • Avoid misleading information or fake engagement.
  • Maintain professional quality in campaigns.

2. Campaign Rules

  • Creators must complete campaign requirements.
  • Submit content within the given deadline.
  • Follow campaign instructions provided by Vantara Media.
  • Changes may be requested for quality improvement.

3. Payment Guidelines

  • Payments are processed after campaign verification.
  • Views, engagement and campaign performance may be considered.
  • Payment timelines will be mentioned in campaign details.

4. Account Safety

  • Creators are responsible for their own social accounts.
  • Do not share passwords or sensitive information.
  • Report any suspicious activity to Vantara Media support.
